# CiscoMaster RAP-90 — Marketing Launch Plan

Status: READY FOR OUTREACH APPROVAL
Date: 2026-09-20
Owner: WDC LLC / CiscoMaster

## Objective
Generate qualified CiscoMaster opportunities for UC Health Check, focused assessment, Enterprise assessment, remediation, Assurance, and partner delivery.

## Campaign lanes

### 1. UC Hiring Signal — End Users
Trigger: organization is actively recruiting Cisco UC / CUCM / Voice / Collaboration expertise.
Positioning: WDC does not replace the hire or internal team. CiscoMaster provides immediate Cisco Certified engineering capacity for defined assessment/project needs while the organization builds or expands its team.
Landing page: /capacity/
UTM: utm_source=hiring_signal&utm_medium=outbound&utm_campaign=uc_capacity_2026

### 2. UC Hiring Signal — Partners / MSPs / Integrators
Trigger: partner is recruiting collaboration engineers, managed-service engineers, contact-center engineers, or project delivery specialists.
Positioning: WDC can provide approved overflow/specialist delivery capacity while the partner retains the customer relationship.
Landing page: /partners/
UTM: utm_source=partner_hiring_signal&utm_medium=outbound&utm_campaign=partner_capacity_2026

### 3. Direct End User — Health / Lifecycle / Upgrade
Trigger: known Cisco UC estate, upgrade, lifecycle, migration, operational-risk or staffing pressure.
Positioning: independent engineer-reviewed assessment, not an automated scanner.
Landing page: /health-check/ or /enterprise/
UTM: utm_source=direct_outbound&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=uc_health_2026

## Qualification rules
Prioritize:
- Active posting within ~60 days.
- CUCM explicitly named.
- Broader stack named: Unity, UCCX/UCCE, IM&P/Jabber, Expressway, CUBE/gateways, Webex, SBC, contact center.
- Multi-site, healthcare, public-sector, enterprise, or managed-service environment.
- Posting mentions upgrades, migrations, lifecycle, documentation, vendor coordination, third-party integrations, on-call pressure, or project backlog.
- Clear non-clearance commercial/public-sector fit.

Deprioritize:
- Clearance-only federal roles where WDC cannot realistically deliver.
- Recruiter-only listings with no identifiable end customer.
- Roles that are primarily desktop/mobile support with little UC engineering.
- Expired postings unless they reveal a strong persistent UC estate and another current signal exists.

## Outreach sequence
Day 0: short hiring-signal email; acknowledge they are building the team; offer defined capacity.
Day 3-4: one follow-up with specific capability relevant to the posting.
Day 9-12: final low-pressure follow-up offering Quick Scan or focused conversation.
Stop after three touches unless prospect engages.

## Offer hierarchy
1. Free UC Quick Scan — low-friction first signal.
2. Focused Assessment — narrow problem or smaller environment.
3. Professional Health Check — from $5,995.
4. Enterprise UC Health Check — custom.
5. Remediation — separately scoped and authorized.
6. UC Assurance — recurring health/lifecycle/change visibility.

## Guardrails
- Never imply WDC knows confidential details from a job posting.
- Never position WDC as trying to eliminate an internal role.
- Do not claim customer logos, outcomes, statistics, certifications, or capabilities that cannot be substantiated.
- Do not send production-change promises as part of an assessment.
- Customer and partner outreach remain separate.
