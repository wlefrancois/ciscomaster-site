WHY IT EXISTS

Small first step. Useful signal.

Some organizations know they need a full assessment. Others simply need an independent first look before committing budget and engineering time.

The Quick Scan intentionally limits scope. It surfaces selected indicators and gives WDC enough evidence to recommend whether the environment warrants deeper investigation.

WHAT IT IS NOT

Professional for free.

The Quick Scan does not include the full technical workbook, complete architecture analysis, topology deliverable, detailed remediation planning or comprehensive Professional assessment scope.

If the scan shows that deeper assessment is warranted, you decide whether to continue. There is no requirement to purchase another service.
