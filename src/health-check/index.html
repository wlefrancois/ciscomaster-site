WHAT IT IS

An operational assessment—not another automated scan.

CiscoMaster collects approved evidence, analyzes supported health and risk indicators, and puts the results through engineering review before they become customer findings.

The goal is to leave both engineering and leadership with a defensible picture of current condition, priority risks and what deserves attention next.

01 / SCOPE

Broad enough to understand the environment. Focused enough to stay actionable.

Exact depth follows the deployed systems, available evidence and agreed engagement scope.

PLATFORMHealth & availability

Cluster, node, service, database, replication and supported operational indicators.

Version exposure, aging components, dependencies and upgrade-readiness observations.

TRUSTCertificates

Certificate conditions and related operational exposure visible from supported evidence.

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO

Assessment and remediation stay separate.

The Professional Health Check does not authorize WDC to make production changes, perform upgrades, replace certificates, redesign the dial plan or implement remediation.

When findings require action, remediation is separately scoped, planned, authorized and validated.
