02 / CHANGE CONTROL

Production work gets its own controls.

Assessment findings do not automatically authorize changes.

  1. 01ValidateConfirm the finding and current-state condition.
  2. 02PlanDefine implementation, prerequisites, risk and rollback.
  3. 03AuthorizeCustomer approves the agreed production work and window.
  4. 04ImplementExecute the approved change under the agreed controls.
  5. 05ValidateConfirm expected state and document completion.
03 / DELIVERY

WDC can lead, assist, or work beside your engineers.

The remediation model can adapt to the customer's internal UC capability and change-management process.

Customer-led. WDC supplies specialist engineering support while the internal team executes.

WDC-led. WDC performs the specifically authorized technical scope.

Joint delivery. WDC and the customer team work the change together and validate the result.
